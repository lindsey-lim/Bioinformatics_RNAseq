### Description of Experimental Design

This study works with *Candida albicans*, which is a commensal yeast that can cause a variety of pathologies, and researches how growth conditions affect gene expression and the metabolic responses to thiamine limited conditions. This study looks at the growth of *Candida albicans* under 2 treatments: grown in the presence of thiamine (Thi+) and grown in absence of thiamine (Thi-). There were three replicate samples under each treatment, and I will be looking at the WTA1 sample.
